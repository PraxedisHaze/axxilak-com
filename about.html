The shop and the forge

Small tools. Strange rooms. Useful magic.

Axxilak Studios is an app store and custom app design offering. The shop holds small instruments you can own outright. The forge is where we shape a more particular tool around a real problem.

The point

Software should feel like an instrument, not a landlord.

We make focused tools that do one job well, keep the user in control, and tell the truth about what they do. No rented identity. No attention trap. No vague promise hiding behind a launch video.

The current catalog includes privacy-first research memory, screenshot capture, visual electricity, and browser-based line art. Each product is a room with its own character. The house still has one rule: the person who uses the tool should be able to understand and keep it.

Shape the smallest instrument

We prototype the narrowest useful tool, with a clear boundary and a real first-run path. The goal is a capability you can use, not a dashboard you have to manage.

03

Leave with something you can keep

When the instrument earns its place, we make the handoff explicit: what it stores, where it runs, what it costs, and what remains yours.
