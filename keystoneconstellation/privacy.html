Privacy Policy

Short Version

We do not silently collect your data.

If we ever want data beyond what is strictly required to complete your order or reply to your message, we will ask you directly first.

In the absence of an active, explicit yes, we operate under an active no.

What We Collect

Keystone Constellation does not run tracking, profiling, behavioral analytics, or advertising identifiers on this site.

When you purchase a product, payment and order handling are processed by our merchant platform. We receive only the minimum practical order details needed to deliver support and fulfill your request (for example, purchase identity and contact email).

When you email us directly, we receive what you send in that email and use it only to respond to you.

Cookies, Analytics, and Pixels

We do not use ad pixels. We do not run behavioral tracking. We do not run surveillance analytics on your activity.

Data Use, Sharing, and Selling

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not rent your personal information.

We do not share your information with third parties for marketing.

Your Control

If you want us to delete any message history you have sent us by email, request it at t.drake@keystoneconstellation.com and we will honor it unless we are required by law to retain a record.

Policy Changes

If our data practices ever change, we will update this page first.
