What you're actually buying, before you click

  • A ZIP file, not a Chrome Web Store listing. You extract it, turn on your browser's Developer Mode, and click Load Unpacked - about two minutes, no coding, step-by-step instructions included in the download.
  • Works in Chrome, Brave, Edge, and other Chromium browsers. One purchase covers every device and browser profile you personally own or control - not locked to a single machine.
  • Updates are manual, not automatic. Because it isn't a Web Store install, a new version means re-downloading and reloading the folder yourself. Your saved collection carries over as long as you extract into the same folder and click Reload, not Remove - full steps ship in the download.
  • The Shared Local Library is optional and separate. A small companion program (needs Python 3 installed, Windows-tested) that lets two of your own browsers share one collection over 127.0.0.1. Skip it entirely if you only use one browser.
  • One-time payment, no subscription. The regular price is $20. During this early release, it is $12 — 40% off. No recurring $3/month charge, and no account required to use it.
